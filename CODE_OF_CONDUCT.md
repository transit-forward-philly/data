# Code of Conduct

Transit Forward Philly's data work is open to anyone who wants to help people understand transit in our region. We want it to be a place where people with different skills, backgrounds and views on tools can work well together.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially in reviews. Critique the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and people who are still learning. Everyone started somewhere.
- Respect different ways of contributing. Some people code, some curate data, some review, some use AI tools and some don't. All of it counts.
- Be honest about data. Cite sources, flag uncertainty, and correct mistakes openly.
- Keep private information private. Don't post personal details about anyone without their consent.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing someone's private information
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring requests to stop

## If something goes wrong

Contact a maintainer directly, or Transit Forward Philly. We'll handle reports privately and respond as quickly as we can. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or remove access for people who don't follow this code.

This applies in the repo, its issues and pull requests, and committee meetings and channels.
