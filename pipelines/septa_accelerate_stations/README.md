# SEPTA Accelerate stations to districts

Lists every Regional Rail and Metro station with the service SEPTA's Accelerate plan proposes for it, places each in a Pennsylvania House and Senate district, county, municipality and Philadelphia Council district, and counts each trolley route's stops by district.

- **Run:** `python build.py ../../datasets/septa-region/septa-accelerate-station-service [CACHE_DIR]`, then `python check_with_census_geocoder.py ../../datasets/septa-region/septa-accelerate-station-service/census-geocoder-check.csv`
- **Needs:** `geopandas`. Downloads SEPTA GTFS release `v202611010` from GitHub, Philadelphia's 2024 Council districts from the City's ArcGIS layer, and Census TIGER/Line 2024 polygons (through the helpers in `regional_rail_station_districts`). Files are reused from `CACHE_DIR` if present, otherwise downloaded into it (or into a temporary folder). Nothing large is committed.
- **Also reads:** the Regional Rail station districts, 2025–26 legislators, 2026 election results and the FY27 capital projects table in `analyses/septa-capital-tip-tracker/data/`.
- **Hand-entered rules:** the service tiers per line, the trolley proposals and the station-to-project matches are constants at the top of `build.py`, each with the plan, Master Plan or budget page it comes from. Check those first when the plan or budget is revised.
- **Plan projects:** `python build_plan_projects.py ../../datasets/septa-region/septa-accelerate-station-service [CACHE_DIR]`, after `build.py`. Reads Jeff Maki's catalog in `sources/` and the station file; needs `geopandas`. Station, route and corridor assignments are constants at the top of the script.
- **Frequent transit coverage:** `python build_frequent_coverage.py ../../datasets/septa-region/septa-accelerate-station-service [CACHE_DIR]`, after [`../septa_bus_routes/build.py`](../septa_bus_routes/) and the district summary (it takes its district list from the summary and its frequent routes from the bus routes file). Needs `geopandas` 1.0 or later (for the `dwithin` join). Downloads the Census 2020 redistricting file for Pennsylvania (57 MB, not committed). Rerun the district summary afterward to pick up its two share columns. It also prints the county totals and the two Philadelphia definition checks quoted in the dataset README.
- **Costs:** `python build_cost_sources.py [OUTPUT_CSV]` (defaults to the dataset folder), after `../dvrpc_lrp_septa_projects/build.py`. No extra packages. The typed-in figures, with the wording each comes from, are constants at the top.
- **District summary:** `python build_district_summary.py ../../datasets/septa-region/septa-accelerate-station-service/septa-accelerate-district-summary.csv` (Python 3.11+, no extra packages). It warns on stderr when the coverage file is missing or its districts do not match the summary rows, and leaves those share columns blank. Run after `build.py`, `build_plan_projects.py` and [`../septa_bus_routes/build.py`](../septa_bus_routes/); it reads their output plus the boardings, legislators, election and vote datasets, and downloads nothing.
