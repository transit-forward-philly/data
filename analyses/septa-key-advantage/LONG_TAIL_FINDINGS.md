# Key Advantage beyond the big four

*SEPTA partner list, Sept. 2026; Census LEHD LODES 2023. Built by `analyze_long_tail.py`; industry and ownership coding is in `partner_industry.py`.*

## Summary

Four employers hold 91% of Key Advantage's employer members:

- Penn Medicine, 26,500
- City of Philadelphia, 24,450
- Moravia Health Network, 2,148
- Delaware County, 1,450

That's 54,548 of 60,260. The other 62 employers cover 5,712 people, and the typical one has about 25 members. College student programs (4,635 students) are left out throughout.

## Ownership

Nonprofits and public agencies make up a minority of these 62 partners but cover two-thirds of their members.

| Owner | Partners | Members |
|---|---|---|
| Businesses | 33 | 1,769 (31%) |
| Nonprofits | 22 | 2,292 (40%) |
| Public agencies | 4 | 1,489 (26%) |
| Not identified | 3 | 162 (3%) |

The four public agencies are the Parking Authority, the Pennsylvania House, PHDC and the Supreme Court's Disciplinary Board.

## Location

They are mostly Center City offices. Professional services is the largest industry group, with 15 partners: law firms, consultants, architects and lab start-ups. Of the 54 partners we placed in the city, 40 are in Center City.

## Timing

- 5 partners joined before May 2023, with 712 members.
- 45 joined from May 2023, when the small-business tier opened, through Aug. 2025, with 4,434 members.
- 12 joined after the September 2025 price reset, with 566 members. Small employers kept signing up after the price rose.

## Caveats

- **Coding:** industry and ownership are our coding. Ownership for 9 partners is inferred from what they do rather than confirmed. Three partners are unidentified.
- **Measures:** member counts are not employee counts, and comparing partner industries with job shares shows representation, not demand.
- **Suburban partners:** Swarthmore College's staff and the City Avenue District are outside the city but included above.
